# The Solenne Archive Network — Consolidated Field Records, Vol. VII

*Internal circulation only. Compiled from station logs, personnel files,
equipment provenance appendices, and foundry records. This volume covers
the Solenne Archive Network's six coastal and inland monitoring stations
for the period 2004–2023.*

---

## 1. Network Overview

The Solenne Archive Network (SAN) operates six remote environmental
monitoring stations, each tasked with long-term acoustic, seismic, and
hydrological measurement. The stations are, in order of founding:

- **Station Halvorsen** — inland seismic monitoring, northern basin
- **Station Kessom** — coastal hydrophone array, southern strait
- **Station Priddow** — glacial melt telemetry, high-altitude
- **Station Ferrant** — estuarine salinity monitoring
- **Station Oskelund** — deep-basin pressure monitoring
- **Station Vantrelle** — atmospheric-marine boundary layer study

Each station is staffed on a rotating basis, with technicians typically
serving two-to-four year postings before transferring. The Network
Directorate maintains a central equipment pool, sourced historically
from three main suppliers: the **Draye Foundry**, **Combrent Instruments**,
and **Ostley Precision Works**.

This volume does not attempt to cross-reference every technician
transfer or equipment provenance record in the main text; such details
are distributed across the appendices, where they are organized by
category rather than by station, to keep each appendix focused.

## 4. Equipment Provenance Appendix — Category: Acoustic Instruments

The **Verrin series hydrophones** (Verrin-6 through Verrin-11) were
manufactured by Combrent Instruments between 2005 and 2019. The
Verrin-9 model, deployed at both Kessom and Oskelund, was known for a
recurring diaphragm fatigue issue in units manufactured after 2016,
attributed to a supplier change in the diaphragm polymer.

Combrent's acoustic line was serviced primarily through Ostley
Precision Works for replacement diaphragms, though mounting hardware
for hydrophone buoys was sourced separately, generally from whichever
supplier had surplus stock closest to the deploying station — a
cost-saving practice the Directorate formalized in its 2015 logistics
memo (see §11).

## 5. Equipment Provenance Appendix — Category: Seismic Instruments

The **Ansitrom seismometer array**, installed at Station Halvorsen in
2010, one year after the station's founding, was calibrated using
mounting and tensioning hardware sourced from the **Draye Foundry**,
which at the time was the only supplier certified for the array's
required tensioning tolerances. The calibration was carried out by the
station's incoming instrumentation lead, who had joined the year
before as part of the founding technical staff.

The Ansitrom array remained the primary seismic instrument at
Halvorsen until a partial upgrade in 2019, at which point a secondary
array (the Coblenz-4) was added but the original Ansitrom units were
retained for baseline comparison.

## 8. Foundry History — Draye Foundry

The Draye Foundry has supplied mounting and tensioning hardware to the
Solenne Archive Network since the network's earliest stations were
established. Company records indicate the foundry converted its
primary production line from hot-forged to **cold-forged steel** in
**2009**, a change undertaken to improve fatigue resistance in marine
and seismic tensioning hardware. This conversion coincided, by
coincidence noted in the foundry's own commemorative 25th-anniversary
brochure, with the founding year of Station Halvorsen, one of the
foundry's earliest network clients following the conversion.

Following the 2009 conversion, the foundry's flagship clamp product
line — marketed under the name **Torvek-C** — became the standard
mounting clamp supplied to any station requesting Draye Foundry
hardware, superseding the earlier "Torvek-B" hot-forged line.

The foundry's other product lines (structural brackets, cable
tensioners, and the "Merrow" bolt series) are documented in Appendix
D and are not relevant to clamp specifications.

## 9. Foundry Specification Table — Draye Foundry Clamp Lines (by Standard Revision Year)

| Product Line | Standard Revision Year | Material | Rated Tensile Strength |
|---|---|---|---|
| Torvek-A | 1998 | Hot-forged mild steel | 9.6 kN |
| Torvek-B | 2003 | Hot-forged alloy steel | 11.8 kN |
| **Torvek-C** | **2009** | **Cold-forged steel** | **14.2 kN** |
| Torvek-C2 | 2014 | Cold-forged steel, revised coating | 14.5 kN |
| Torvek-D | 2021 | Cold-forged stainless composite | 16.0 kN |

Note: the "Standard Revision Year" indicates the year a given
product's manufacturing standard was adopted, not the year any
individual clamp unit was manufactured. Clamps continue to be
produced under a given standard until superseded — Torvek-C units,
for instance, were manufactured continuously from 2009 until the
Torvek-C2 revision in 2014, after which older Torvek-C surplus stock
remained in circulation at various stations for years afterward,
still stamped with the original 2009 standard rating.

## 10. Equipment Provenance Appendix — Category: Surplus and Redistribution

The Directorate's central equipment pool periodically redistributes
surplus stock between stations when a given station upgrades its
hardware standard. Notable redistributions include:

- Halvorsen's surplus Torvek-C stock (remaining after the 2014
  transition to Torvek-C2 hardware for new installations) was flagged
  as available for network-wide redistribution in a 2015 inventory
  audit.
- Oskelund's Ostley-sourced pressure hardware was never redistributed,
  as it was deemed non-standard for other stations' tensioning
  requirements.
- Priddow's glacial housing hardware, being custom-fabricated, was
  never part of the general surplus pool.

## 11. Directorate Logistics Memo, 2015 (Excerpt)

*"...to reduce shipping costs for routine buoy and mounting hardware
repairs, field teams are hereby authorized to draw mounting clamps
from whichever station's surplus lot is geographically or logistically
nearest, rather than requisitioning new stock from a supplier
directly, provided the surplus hardware meets or exceeds the minimum
tensile rating specified for the application. Field teams must record
the source lot in the maintenance log for traceability..."*

This memo formalized a practice that had, in fact, already been
informally followed at several stations since at least 2012.

## 16. Station Vantrelle — Founding Notes

Station Vantrelle was founded in 2016 as a joint effort between the
incoming Directorate oversight board and a founding technical team

## 17. Glossary of Instrument and Hardware Terms

- **Diaphragm fatigue**: gradual mechanical failure of a hydrophone's
  sensing membrane, often linked to polymer supplier changes.
- **Tensile rating**: the maximum load a mounting clamp is rated to
  withstand before failure, typically expressed in kilonewtons (kN).
- **Surplus lot**: a batch of previously-requisitioned hardware held
  in a station's local inventory and made available for network-wide
  redistribution under the 2015 logistics memo.
- **Standard revision**: a formal update to a foundry's manufacturing
  specification, after which newly produced units carry the revised
  rating, though older stock retains its original stamped rating.
